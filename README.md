# Somatosensory Brainstem Auditory Evoked Potentials (BAEP / ABR) Skill

A production-grade, zero-mock personal agent skill engineered for neurophysiological auditory brainstem conduction quantification, retrocochlear lesion screening, and brainstem pathway profiling.

## Features
- **Deterministic Latency & IPL Derivation**: Accurately computes $I-III$, $III-V$, and $I-V$ central conduction intervals.
- **Interaural Asymmetry & Amplitude Ratio Auditing**: Flags subtle unilateral acoustic nerve conduction delays.
- **Pure Standard Library**: Zero external dependencies; fully portable for edge and clinical AI deployment.

## Clinical References
- American Clinical Neurophysiology Society (ACNS): *Guideline 9B: Guidelines for clinical auditory evoked potentials*. Journal of Clinical Neurophysiology, 23(2), 125-137.
- Chiappa, K. H. (1997). *Evoked potentials in clinical medicine*. Lippincott-Raven.
